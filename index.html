      1. Anatomical Modeling Scope & Epistemic Transparency

      To uphold scientific integrity, this atlas distinguishes between three tiers of anatomical representation:

      • Directly Modeled (Morphometric): External tagmata (head capsule, pro-/meso-/metathorax, 10-segmented abdomen), Comstock–Needham wing venation ($Sc, R_1\text{–}R_5, M_1\text{–}M_3, CuA_1\text{–}CuA_2, 1A+2A, 3A$, and discal cell), sexually dimorphic male $CuA_2$ androconial patch, clubbed antennae, and nymphalid reduced prothoracic forelegs are constructed directly from published morphometric proportions of Danaus plexippus (Ehrlich, 1958).
      • Simplified 3D Reconstruction: Indirect flight musculature (dorsolongitudinal muscles $DLMs$ and dorsoventral muscles $DVMs$) and endocuticular phragmata represent volumetric functional blocks demonstrating thoracic indirect flight mechanics.
      • Schematic Topographical Organ Models: Internal visceral systems (digestive tract, Malpighian tubules, dorsal tubular heart, supra-/subesophageal and ventral ganglia, tracheal trunks, and male/female reproductive tracts) are 3D schematic reconstructions positioned according to dissecting-microscope atlases (Eaton, 1988; Scoble, 1992). They are not derived from micro-CT volumetric scans.

      Wing Kinematics Note: The interactive wing-flapping engine models coupled three-axis Euler rotations (flapping elevation/depression, spanwise pronation/supination feathering, and lead-lag sweep) alongside thoraco-abdominal pitch counter-oscillation at ~10 Hz (Betts & Wootton, 1988). While biologically grounded in free-flight high-speed cinematography of papilionoid butterflies, the waveform is an analytical kinematic synthesis rather than raw motion-capture telemetry.
